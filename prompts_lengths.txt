long250	Write a detailed technical review of tidal physics and tidal prediction. Begin with the gravitational potential of the Moon and the Sun and derive the tidal force as the gradient of that potential, explaining why there are two tidal bulges. Then discuss the equilibrium tide, the dynamic tide, the role of the Coriolis force and amphidromic systems, and why real coastal tides can be metres high while the open-ocean tide is tens of centimetres. Cover spring and neap tides, perigean and apogean cycles, and the 18.6-year nodal modulation. Explain how harmonic analysis decomposes a tide-gauge record into constituents, how the constituents are used for prediction, and what limits forecast accuracy. Discuss storm surge, sea-level rise, and the interaction of tides with estuaries and tidal turbines. Include concrete numbers and identify the open questions in tidal science.
