history_of_computing	Provide a comprehensive history of computing from mechanical calculators to modern artificial intelligence. Cover key inventions, the evolution of programming languages, operating systems, networking, and the major figures and companies involved. Explain the technical ideas behind each era in accessible language and discuss how these developments shaped society.
evolution_of_language	Explain how human language evolved and how languages change over time. Cover theories of language origins, the biological basis of speech, phonology, syntax, and semantics, and the mechanisms of language change such as borrowing, sound shifts, and grammaticalization. Provide examples from multiple language families.
renewable_energy_economics	Analyze the economics of renewable energy. Cover the levelized cost of electricity for solar and wind, storage, grid integration, subsidies, and market design. Explain how learning curves and manufacturing scale have driven cost declines, and discuss the remaining barriers to deep decarbonization.
history_of_mathematics	Provide a detailed history of mathematics from ancient Mesopotamia and Greece through the Islamic Golden Age, the European Renaissance, and modern abstract mathematics. Explain the key problems, proofs, and the people who solved them, and how notation and rigor developed over time.
astronomy_observations	Explain how astronomers observe the universe across the electromagnetic spectrum and beyond. Cover telescopes, spectroscopy, gravitational waves, neutrinos, and space missions. Describe what these observations have revealed about stars, galaxies, dark matter, dark energy, and the early universe.
