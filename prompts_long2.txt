ocean_currents	Explain how ocean currents work and why they matter for climate. Cover wind-driven circulation, thermohaline circulation, the Coriolis effect, upwelling, El Nino and La Nina, and the role of the oceans in storing heat and carbon. Describe the observational methods used to map currents.
history_of_computing	Provide a comprehensive history of computing from mechanical calculators to modern artificial intelligence. Cover key inventions, the evolution of programming languages, operating systems, networking, and the major figures and companies involved. Explain the technical ideas behind each era in accessible language and discuss how these developments shaped society.
urban_transportation	Analyze the design of urban transportation systems. Cover public transit, road networks, congestion, land use, cycling and pedestrian infrastructure, and the economics of transport investment. Discuss how cities can reduce emissions and travel time.
astronomy_observations	Explain how astronomers observe the universe across the electromagnetic spectrum and beyond. Cover telescopes, spectroscopy, gravitational waves, neutrinos, and space missions. Describe what these observations have revealed about stars, galaxies, dark matter, dark energy, and the early universe.
linguistics_syntax	Explain modern syntactic theory. Cover phrase structure, constituency, dependency, movement, binding, and the difference between competence and performance. Discuss cross-linguistic variation and the main competing frameworks.
climate_models	Explain how climate models work. Cover radiative transfer, ocean and atmosphere dynamics, grid resolution, parameterization of clouds, coupling, and the use of ensembles. Discuss how models are evaluated and where uncertainty comes from.
