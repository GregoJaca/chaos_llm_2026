ocean_currents	Explain how ocean currents work and why they matter for climate. Cover wind-driven circulation, thermohaline circulation, the Coriolis effect, upwelling, El Nino and La Nina, and the role of the oceans in storing heat and carbon. Describe the observational methods used to map currents.
history_of_music	Provide a history of Western music from medieval chant through the Renaissance, Baroque, Classical, Romantic, and twentieth-century styles. Explain the development of harmony, notation, instruments, and performance practice, and discuss how social and technological changes shaped musical life.
software_engineering_practices	Explain modern software engineering practices in depth. Cover version control, testing strategies, continuous integration, code review, design patterns, refactoring, observability, and incident response. Discuss trade-offs and common failure modes in large codebases.
sleep_and_memory	Explain the relationship between sleep and memory. Cover sleep stages, consolidation of declarative and procedural memories, the role of the hippocampus and cortex, dream function theories, and the effects of sleep deprivation. Discuss what the evidence does and does not support.
volcanoes_and_plate_tectonics	Explain how volcanoes work and how they relate to plate tectonics. Cover magma generation, eruption styles, hazards, prediction methods, and the role of volcanism in the carbon cycle and mass extinctions. Provide examples from different tectonic settings.
urban_transportation	Analyze the design of urban transportation systems. Cover public transit, road networks, congestion, land use, cycling and pedestrian infrastructure, and the economics of transport investment. Discuss how cities can reduce emissions and travel time.
